Overview

Hospitals can be overwhelming for children, often heightening anxiety during treatment. Our team addressed this matter by programming MiRo-E robot (from consequential robotics) to act as a therapeutic companion, guiding young patients through calming activities such as breathing exercises, storytelling, and interactive standby.

Concept

To program MiRo-E to be a therapeutic companion, we included the following features:

  • Custom activities to reduce stress and encourage relaxation: guided breathing, storytelling, muscle relaxation and dance
  • Trigger activities using one of these methods: QR (Aruco) Code, audio recognition or remote control
  • Touch, sound, and vision sensors to respond to children in real time during interactive standby

Achievements

  • Developed modular software using Python, ROS, and OpenCV within an MVC architecture on Ubuntu.
  • Implemented multithreading to enable concurrent sensor input, robot movement, audio processing, and face detection.
  • Integrated libraries such as Pillow and OpenCV to support real-time GUI tracking and video processing for QR code detection and face recognition
  • Collaborated with health care professional (sponsor) to ensure project meets real-world needs.
  • Reused a Bluetooth ESP32-based remote to enable easier interaction for children.
